# 14. Lower Specification Builder Content Visible in Screenshot

### SECTION 4.0: Quality Assurance, Testing & Acceptance Criteria

1. Internal Hydrostatic Pressure Test: Pipes shall withstand internal hydrostatic pressure test for 100 hours at 20°C and 165 hours at 80°C in accordance with IS 4984:2016 Table 4.

2. Melt Flow Index (MFI): Tested as per IS 2530:1963, variation shall not exceed ±20% of base resin.

3. Carbon Black Content & Dispersion: Grade 3 as per IS 2530:1963.

4. Independent Testing: Inspection by third-party agency (RITES / CIPET / CEIL) from a NABL-accredited test laboratory.

---

### SECTION 5.0: CVC Anti-Tailoring & Fair Competition Clause

In compliance with Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) OM No. 03-05-01 and GFR 2017 guidelines, this tender specification is strictly generic and brand-neutral. No proprietary vendor parameters, trade makes, or brand names are mandated. Any bidder offering products possessing valid BIS ISI certification complying with IS 4984:2016 shall be eligible for technical evaluation.

---

### SECTION 6.0: Delivery, Marking & Guarantee

Each pipe length or coil shall be legibly and indelibly marked with: Manufacturer Name/Trade Name, Nominal Size (OD × Wall thickness), Pressure Rating (PN), Material Grade (PE 100), Standard Mark (ISI), and Manufacturing Lot/Year.
